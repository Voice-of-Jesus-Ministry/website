The Feeding Program

How a feeding day unfolds

These photos show a children’s feeding day. Our outreach also serves a jail in Iloilo. Contact us for upcoming dates and ways to help.

1

Everyone gathers

Children settle onto the covered court in one big group — the youngest in front, older kids mixed in beside them, babies carried in by mums and carers.

2

Meals are handed out

Each child receives a filled tray and a juice pouch. Kids carry their own meals to their spot, and helpers move through the group making sure no one is missed.

3

They eat — together

Spoons up, trays open, friends on every side. Adults stay close by, and the meal is unhurried: time to eat well, chat, pose for a photo, and simply be kids.

What's on the tray
