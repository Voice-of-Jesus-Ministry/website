    BIBLE STUDY · MATTHEW 5:14–16

    You Are the Light of the World

    “Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.”

    Matthew 5:14 · King James Version

    A calling to live openly

    In the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus tells His disciples that they are the light of the world. He describes a city on a hill: its light is visible to the people around it. This is a calling to let faith shape everyday life, where others can see its fruit.

    Jesus continues with the picture of a candle placed on a candlestick rather than hidden under a bushel. Its light serves everyone in the house. In the same way, the gifts and opportunities God gives us can become a blessing to our neighbors.

    Good works that point to the Father

    “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.”

    Matthew 5:16 · King James Version

    The purpose of being seen is to bring glory to our Father in heaven. Our actions can make His goodness visible: sharing a meal, listening patiently, telling the truth, forgiving someone, or helping a person who feels forgotten. These ordinary acts of love give faith a practical expression.

    Put it into practice

    Think of one person you will meet today. What would kindness, honesty, or patient service look like in that relationship? Choose one concrete action and follow through without asking for recognition.

    Reflect and pray

    Where am I hiding an opportunity to do good? How can my actions help someone recognize the Father's care?

    Father, help me live with the light Jesus calls me to share. Give me courage to do good, humility to serve, and love that directs attention to You. Amen.
