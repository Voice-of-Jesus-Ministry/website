## Recommended payment setup after qualification is established
Use a verified nonprofit fundraiser/check-out in the corporation’s exact name, rather than redirecting donors to an individual’s fundraiser while promising a deduction. The existing GoFundMe platform offers nonprofit fundraisers with provider-issued receipts; enrollment, beneficiary verification and an organization-controlled bank/payout arrangement still need to be completed. Review provider terms and fees before enrollment. Do not paste bank credentials or identity documents into chat or Git.

Before activation, verify the beneficiary, the EIN, the public fundraiser type, permitted uses of funds, payout control, refund handling, and the provider’s receipt behavior. Retain board authorization and donation accounting. Funding overseas outreach requires the corporation to retain appropriate oversight; do not assume an unrestricted pass-through to an individual establishes deductibility.

## Receipt requirements to implement
When eligibility and the payment arrangement are confirmed, acknowledgment records should include legal name/EIN, donor name, gift date, cash amount (or a description of noncash property without assigning a value), and the applicable goods/services statement. Do not automatically assert that no goods or services were provided if that is not true. A donor needs a contemporaneous written acknowledgment for a gift of $250 or more. Reconcile refunds and recurring gifts and prevent duplicate receipts. Prefer provider-issued receipts if the provider is the legal recipient; do not issue a conflicting duplicate from the ministry.
